Mail Organizer

A personal, non-commercial tool that one student uses to organise their own college email and schedule.

It runs only for its owner's own Google account and is not offered to anyone else. It:

  • sorts the owner's Gmail into labels,
  • reads the owner's Google Classroom courses (read-only),
  • adds deadlines, classes and exams to a calendar that the tool itself creates,
  • copies academic notes and assignments into the owner's own Notion workspace.

It never deletes email and never shares or sells any data. See the privacy policy.

Personal project · not affiliated with Google, Notion or any institution.
