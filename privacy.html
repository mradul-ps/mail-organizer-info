Privacy Policy: Mail Organizer

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who uses it

Mail Organizer is a personal tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: reads email metadata and content, and adds or removes labels. It never deletes or sends email.
  • Google Calendar: only the calendar that the app itself creates. It cannot see or change any other calendar.
  • Google Classroom: read-only access to the owner's courses, coursework and course materials.

How the data is used

  • Email text is sent to the Google Gemini API only to classify it, summarise it and find dates.
  • Summaries, deadlines and links are written to the owner's own Notion workspace and the app's own calendar.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model by this app.
  • Login tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and in encrypted secrets of the owner's private repository.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mail Organizer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
